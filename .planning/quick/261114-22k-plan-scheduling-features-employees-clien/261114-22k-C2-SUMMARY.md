## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug prevention] Per-session cap on per-visit payments.** The spec said total paid versus accrued. With a total-only cap, paying two visits' worth against one visit and then correcting the other (unpaid) completed visit back to booked drives balance negative, and the C1 trigger only protects visits that carry a payment. A per-session cap makes paid <= accrued an invariant. Stricter than the spec; a lump sum for several visits needs one payment per visit.

**2. [Rule 2] Cancelled per_visit packages still accept settlement of delivered visits** (upfront and installments are rejected). Otherwise an owed visit could never be paid after a cancel and there is no refund flow.

**3. [Rule 3] Ledger cannot import the clinic helpers.** `package-ledger.ts` must stay Node-loadable (no `@/` runtime imports), so `moneyToPaise`, `parseMoneyPaise` and the payment methods are local and the check script asserts equality with `toPaise`, `parseMoneyInput` and `CLINIC_PAYMENT_MODES`. The UI imports the clinic payment modes directly.

**4. [Scope, user-added] Reschedule guard** implemented in the payment migration, not the C1 file. It applies to any live (non-voided) link, including rolled over and forfeited ones, and a null new date counts as expired for a package with an expiry (same rule as attach).

**5. [Rule 2] Readable appointment errors.** `updateAppointment` and `setAppointmentStatus` map the expiry rejection and the payment-pinned rejection to sentences (existing error display in the Appointments tab).

**6. [Rule 2] Replay checked before balance, package existence checked before the advisory lock** (a caller can never lock another tenant's package id).

**7. Scope notes.** The package list cards show the plan and price but not balances (money is loaded only for the drawer to avoid three extra requests per page). Docs were updated in this plan as well.

No Rule 4 items. No auth gates. `.vscode/settings.json`, `.planning/milestone.lock`, `.planning/state.json` and `.planning/quick/261112-*` untouched. STATE.md and ROADMAP.md were not updated (this master plan is a quick task, as for C1).
