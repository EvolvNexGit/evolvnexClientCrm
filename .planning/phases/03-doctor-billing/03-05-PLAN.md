<objective>
A receptionist bills a Completed consultation from the consult screen: patient and fee prefilled, discount and payment chosen, a bill created through the invoker function, and a receipt printable straight away (BILL-02 fields written, BILL-03 function called; decisions D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04).

Purpose: this is the user-visible half of ROADMAP criteria 2 and 4. The price, totals, appointment link, source and status are decided by the database function from plan 03-01; the browser only names a consultation, a patient, products with quantities, a flat discount and a payment choice.
Output: money helpers and the RPC wrapper, the create-bill modal, and the gated Create bill entries in the consultation tab (read-only Completed view and Completed queue rows).

Tracer note: Task 1 is the tracer: the read-only Completed view's Create bill, through the modal, the RPC wrapper and the live function, to a created receipt with Print. Task 2 expands to the Completed queue rows (mobile and desktop) and the already-billed link.

**Prerequisites:** plans 03-01 (function live), 03-03 (exclusion guards live first, so a Clinic bill never alerts) and 03-04 (`useClinicReceiptPrint`).

Out of scope (D-14): voiding a wrong bill (D-05, fix manually), refunds, partial payments, insurance, tax lines, WhatsApp receipt sending.
</objective>
