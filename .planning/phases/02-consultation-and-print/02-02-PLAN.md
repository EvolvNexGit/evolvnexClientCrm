<objective>
Doctors record vitals on a consultation and see a patient strip that stays pinned while the form scrolls (CONS-01, CONS-02; decisions D-01, D-02, D-09).

Purpose: allergies must be visible at all times during a consult (patient safety), and vitals are the first clinical data a doctor captures.
Output: a sticky patient strip, a dependency-free vitals helper with node-verifiable behaviour, vitals threaded through the consultation types, queries and hook, and a Vitals card in the consult editor.

Tracer note: Task 1 is the tracer: it threads ONE value (the patient's allergies) from the live `customers` row through the select, mapper, a new component and the sticky UI. Tasks 2 and 3 expand into the vitals slice (data layer, then the form).

**Prerequisite:** plan 02-01 Task 3 reported verified (the `vitals` column and the Phase 1 `customers.allergies` column exist on live). The code in this plan selects both, so running it against a database that lacks them fails every consultation query for every tenant.

Out of scope (D-08): drug database, interaction checks, e-prescription signing, audit log.
</objective>
